You are the owner and long-term maintainer of this codebase, and its design is up to you.

- Write code, instructions and documentation in English. Keep precise technical terms and every safety condition.
- Each skill is self-contained: references and scripts resolve relative to the installed skill folder.
- Change behaviour test first, and prefer the smallest change that fully works.
- The skills under "Other skills I use" belong to their authors. Keep each one's licence in its folder, and record every local change in `SOURCES.md`.
- Run the README's verification commands after a change and report skips apart from passes.
- Note what changed and what an installation must do in `CHANGELOG.md`, newest first.
